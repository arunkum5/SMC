Concrete Grades

M7.5 TO M80

Quality Concrete for Every Construction Requirement

We supply Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) in a wide range of grades, from M7.5 to M80, suitable for residential, commercial, industrial and infrastructure projects.

The "M" indicates the concrete mix, while the number represents its characteristic compressive strength in MPa (N/mm²) at 28 days.

Ready-Mix Concrete Delivered to Your Site

Get the required concrete grade mixed and delivered directly to your construction site using our concrete mixer trucks. We help ensure consistent quality, timely delivery and the right concrete grade for your project.
